Important Policies

  • For 2-Tier Cakes, minimum weight must be 2 Pounds.
  • 150 Tk Advance payment is required to confirm 2-tier or big orders.
  • Delivery Charge is to be paid by the customer.
  • For Customized Cakes, please pre-order at least 2 Days in advance.
  • Additional payment required for customised cakes minimum 100Tk.
  • We also take urgent orders for Regular Cakes (subject to availability).

About Us

The Story Behind Every Sweet Bite!

Welcome to The Jannat Bakehouse! I'm Jannat, the heart and hands behind every delicious creation. My journey into baking started from a simple passion for making people happy through sweet treats. I believe a cake is more than just dessert; it's a centerpiece for celebrations, a token of love, and a sweet memory in the making.

At Jannat Bakehouse, we pride ourselves on using only the freshest, high-quality ingredients, ensuring every cake is a masterpiece of taste and design. From classic flavors to unique custom creations, we bake with love and attention to detail, just for you. Join us in making your special moments even sweeter!
